Meet the Builder

Third Generation.
Family First.

I'm Stephen Sandlin. My grandfather founded Sandlin Homes decades ago in DFW, and I grew up on job sites learning what it means to build something with permanence. After more than a decade in the industry, I launched Sandlin Signature to build custom and spec homes the way I believe they should be built.

Home for us is Argyle, where my wife, Michelle, and I are raising our two kids, Theo and Sophie. You'll sometimes find them on a job site with me. They're the reason I build the way I do: every home I put my name on is one I'd be proud to raise my own family in.

Sandlin Signature is small on purpose. I'm the one walking your lot, answering your questions, and on site checking the work. Premium finishes. Honest pricing. Real supervision on every build. No shortcuts.
